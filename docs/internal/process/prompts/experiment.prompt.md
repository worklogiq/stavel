Use EXPERIMENT for standalone experiments, proofs of concept and bounded learning. State the hypothesis or learning objective, relevant constraints, environment and stopping condition. Match evidence to the purpose: use observations and examples for learning/POCs, and the [research guide](../../dev/research.guide.md), independent references, baselines and metrics for empirical/mechanism claims. Keep experiments separate from production state and data as the task requires.

Execute only authorized work. Preserve failures, raw measurements needed for reproduction, and limitations. Do not substitute a different mechanism when evaluating the specified one. Do not promote results or rewrite accepted requirements automatically.

Result: observed findings, learning or measurements, interpretation, uncertainty and a useful next step. DESIGN can consume these results; an experiment need not proceed to product implementation. Conformance and demonstrated value are separate claims.
