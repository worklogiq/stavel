Read the [research guide](../../dev/research.guide.md) and relevant contract. State the hypothesis, required mechanism, independent reference, baseline, metrics, environment, and stopping condition. Keep experiments separate from production state and data as the task requires.

Execute only authorized work. Preserve failures, raw measurements needed for reproduction, and limitations. Do not substitute a different mechanism when evaluating the specified one. Do not promote results or rewrite accepted requirements automatically.

Result: measured observations, interpretation, uncertainty, and a recommendation to accept, revise, reject, or investigate further. Conformance and demonstrated value are separate claims.
