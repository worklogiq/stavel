Read the original requirements, required mechanisms, accepted decisions, applicable philosophies, and actual diff. Do not rely exclusively on the implementing assistant's summary.

For each important requirement, find implementation and evidence. Check omissions, substitutions, compatibility, side effects, missing measurements, and test independence. Identify both doc/code discrepancies and potentially incorrect documentation. Structural checks prove only structural properties.

Report findings by impact with locations, evidence, and the smallest useful next action. Distinguish confirmed defects, unverified risks, and questions. State what was checked and what remains unknown. Do not claim complete compliance from self-audit alone.

Keep review read-only unless fixes are authorized. Completion of review is distinct from approval to merge, release, or deploy.
