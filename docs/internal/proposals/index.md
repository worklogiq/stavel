Keep unaccepted ideas, alternative designs, and experiment notes distinct from accepted capability contracts. Use the [research guide](../dev/research.guide.md) and [experiment template](../templates/execution/experiment.template.md).

Every proposal identifies its question, evidence, and decision needed. Promotion into canonical requirements requires an actual authorized decision and links to the supporting record. A proposal does not gain authority from being copied into a summary.
