Capabilities own coherent product responsibilities and their features/workflows. Their requirements and design link shared [contract topics](../contracts/index.md); active execution packets live at the owner they change.

- [Skill](skill/index.md): agent-facing behavior, with [guided workflows](skill/guided-workflows/index.md) as its current feature.
- [CLI](cli/index.md): implemented tooling features and shared services, with explicit planned additions.

Create deeper feature or workflow homes when there is real owning work. The [hierarchy model](../../process/documentation-hierarchy.design.md) supports meaningful nesting without repeating product identity or creating empty future capability trees.
